Achievement

Sri Lanka Robotics Challenge

  • Date: 2019-01-19
  • Organizer: ENTC, University of Moratuwa
  • Venue: University of Moratuwa

Sri Lankan Robotics Challenge (SLRC) is an all-island annual Robotics competition organized by the ENTC, University of Moratuwa. The competition consisted of two tasks: one robot collecting coins in a linear maze and another stationary robot removing obstacles from the path. Our robots, "The Flying Dutchman" and "Kraken" were the finalists in the competition.
