ACES Coders v7.0

  • Date: 2020-02-28 & 29
  • Organizer: Association of Computer Engineering Students
  • Venue: University of Peradeniya

The ACES Coders v7.0 Sri Lanka’s Biggest Programming Competition took place on 15th December 2018. The competition is an overnight 12-hour Algorithmic Programming contest. Our team was ranked 18th, out of 360 teams from universities and institutes island-wide.
