Achievement

XbotiX 2018 Robotics Competition

  • Date: 2018-11-07
  • Organizer: Electrical and Information Engineering Society (EIES) and the Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering Students' Society (MMESS) in collaboration with the IEEE Student Branch and the IET Ruhuna Chapter
  • Venue: University of Ruhuna

‘XbotiX-Robotics Challenge 2018', the annual robotics competition was held at the Faculty of Engineering, University of Ruhuna. More than 50 teams representing public and private universities and schools competed in the undergraduate and school categories, respectively. Our robot, Minotaur, made the final 6 teams out of over 35 teams.
