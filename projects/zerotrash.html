Zero Trash

Zero Trash is a smart waste management platform developed to improve the efficiency of garbage collection and promote sustainable waste management practices in Sri Lanka. The project was designed to address the growing challenges of urban waste generation by providing a digital platform that connects households with garbage collectors, enabling efficient waste collection while supporting recycling initiatives and the development of a circular economy.

The platform provides separate web interfaces for households and garbage collectors. Registered users can request waste pickups through the system, while collection requests are automatically assigned to available garbage collectors. Collectors receive notifications and manage assigned tasks through the application, improving communication and reducing delays in waste collection. To improve accessibility, the platform also integrates an SMS notification service, allowing users without smartphones to receive collection updates.

The system streamlines communication between residents and waste collectors by providing a centralized platform for scheduling, tracking, and managing waste collection requests. By improving collection efficiency and encouraging the proper segregation and transportation of recyclable materials, the platform aims to reduce landfill waste, support recycling activities, and create additional income opportunities for both households and garbage collectors.

The project involved designing and developing the web application, implementing user management and pickup scheduling, integrating notification services, and developing the backend logic required to automate the waste collection workflow. The solution demonstrates how digital technologies can be applied to improve public services while contributing to environmental sustainability and smarter urban waste management.
