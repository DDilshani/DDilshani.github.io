Pera Knowledge Hub

Pera Knowledge Hub is a digital repository developed to preserve, organize, and improve access to academic publications produced at the University of Peradeniya. The project addresses the lack of a centralized digital platform for storing research papers, project reports, and theses, which were previously available primarily as hard copies, limiting their accessibility to researchers, students, and the wider academic community.

The platform enables University of Peradeniya members to register through a content management system (CMS) and upload research papers, project reports, and theses in PDF format. Uploaded documents are securely stored in a centralized repository, providing long-term preservation and easy access to valuable academic resources.

To improve document retrieval, the system automatically extracts text from uploaded PDF documents and indexes their contents within the database. This enables users to perform full-text searches, allowing documents to be discovered not only by their titles or metadata but also by the content contained within the documents. The search engine returns the most relevant publications based on the user's search query, significantly improving the efficiency of information retrieval.

The project involved developing the web-based repository, implementing user authentication and document management features, integrating automated PDF text extraction and indexing, and designing a content-based search engine for efficient document retrieval. The platform demonstrates how information retrieval technologies can be applied to improve knowledge sharing, digital preservation, and accessibility of academic research within higher education institutions.

Key Responsibilities
* Developed a web-based platform for managing and publishing academic documents.
* Implemented secure user registration and authentication for university members.
* Enabled PDF document upload, storage, and metadata management.
* Designed a content management system (CMS) for document submission and publication.
* Participated in system design, implementation, testing, and deployment.

Technologies
Spring Boot, MySQL, Apache PDFBox, HTML, CSS, JavaScrip, Bootstrap, Git
